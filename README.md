# AI Chatbot Response Evaluation for Small Businesses

**Portfolio project by Feyifunmi Omwade Elizabeth**  
**Focus:** AI response quality, business usefulness, factual grounding, and structured evaluation.

## Project overview

This portfolio demonstration shows a repeatable way to assess AI chatbot responses to realistic small-business tasks. It focuses on whether an answer is accurate, relevant to the business owner's request, clear, practical, and supported by the information provided.

The examples use fictional business details and illustrative response evaluations. They are independent portfolio work, not client work and not an official Turing assignment.

## Business scenarios

1. **Customer support:** Draft a response to a delayed-order complaint while respecting a store's refund policy.
2. **Sales analysis:** Interpret a small fictional sales table without inventing causes or unsupported trends.
3. **Marketing planning:** Create a practical one-week promotion plan within a small business's stated budget and capacity.

## Evaluation workflow

1. Read the task prompt and all supplied business context.
2. Extract the owner's goal, constraints, facts, and required output.
3. Review each chatbot response against the same criteria.
4. Flag unsupported claims, missing constraints, ambiguous recommendations, and arithmetic errors.
5. Assign criterion-level scores and explain the evidence behind each score.
6. Compare responses, identify the strongest answer for the use case, and suggest specific improvements.

## Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | What is checked |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Facts, calculations, and policy statements are correct |
| Grounding | Claims are supported by the prompt or supplied files |
| Relevance | The response directly addresses the business owner's goal |
| Clarity | The answer is easy to understand and well organized |
| Actionability | Recommendations include realistic next steps |
| Constraint adherence | Budget, tone, policy, format, and other requirements are followed |
| Risk awareness | The answer avoids promises or assumptions that could harm the business |

Scores use a 1–5 scale, where **1 = poor**, **3 = adequate with notable gaps**, and **5 = strong**. See [the rubric](evaluation-rubric.md) for scoring anchors and [the sample case study](case-study-customer-support.md) for a worked example.

## Important note on evidence

The worked case studies are **method demonstrations**. The sample response patterns, scores, and visual layout specifications are illustrative, not claims that named AI products were tested in a live benchmark or that licensed external assets were sourced. For real work, save the exact prompt, tool/model name, date, full response transcript, asset URL, creator, license/terms, and evidence for each decision.
